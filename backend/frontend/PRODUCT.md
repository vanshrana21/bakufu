# Product context

## Register
Product — analyst workstation, not a marketing page.

## Platform
Web. Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn-style Radix components, Mapbox, Recharts, Zustand, Lucide.

## Users and purpose
MOIL-oriented analysts inspect prospectivity and production risk. Non-technical SIH judges must follow evidence to a reviewable decision at 1080p. The source document ends before Phase 3; Ghost Reserve inventory is an explicit mocked extension.

## Principles
- Light enterprise shell, dark satellite canvas, precise tabular values.
- Keep raw score, masking, geographic scope and recoverability separate.
- Expose fixture/live provenance; unknown and unavailable never mean zero.
- A slag heap inside the belt is not proof of economically recoverable manganese.
- Preserve user context during selection and mask changes; no server-global state.

## Anti-goals
Fabricated reserves, fake operational metrics, unverified national coverage, automatic equipment operation, ornamental hero animations.
